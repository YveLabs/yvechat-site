What you need

A short checklist.

YveChat runs local models through a component on your computer. Six things get you started.

  1. A supported desktop computer

    Windows, macOS, or Linux with enough memory for the models you choose.

    01

  2. YveChat Engine

    The connector that links the hosted workspace to your machine. Free to download and run; currently an unsigned developer preview, so your operating system will warn you before it starts.

    02

  3. Node.js 24 or newer

    The developer preview runs on Node.js. Install it before the Engine, or the launcher will stop and tell you to.

    03

  4. Ollama or another configured runtime

    Ollama is the primary supported runtime. vLLM and OpenAI-compatible endpoints have experimental support.

    04

  5. At least one downloaded model

    Pull a model with Ollama before you begin. Availability depends on what you install.

    05

  6. A compatible browser with local-network permission

    A current browser that can reach your local runtime once you grant permission. Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Firefox are verified. Safari is not, and can open YveChat directly at 127.0.0.1:3210 instead.

    06

Getting started

Four steps to your first local model.
