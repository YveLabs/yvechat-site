What the newsroom is for

The newsroom is the running record of the company: founder interviews, major product updates, partnerships, research, milestones, and announcements. Each story is a single Markdown file, so publishing stays fast and the writing stays close to the work.

Everything you see here is styled to match the rest of the site. Headings use the display serif, body copy uses the same measure as the product pages, and links carry the house green.

Sovereignty is the promise. The newsroom is where we show the receipts.
